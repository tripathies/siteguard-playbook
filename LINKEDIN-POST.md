## 25 Security Checks Before You Ship a Website 🔐

AI-assisted development has made it possible to build a working website in hours. But a successful build is not evidence of secure authentication, authorization, data isolation or operational resilience.

I have organised a practical, evidence-led playbook into two gates:

**1. Website Security**
25 controls covering identity, authorization, APIs, injection, infrastructure, cryptography, data protection, software supply chain and monitoring.

**2. Website Launch Readiness**
16 checks covering DNS, HTTPS, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, Google Search Console, GA4, SEO, privacy, accessibility, performance, backups and rollback.

The repository includes checklists, a bounded passive Python checker, unit tests, CI validation, finding templates and references to OWASP and NIST guidance.

The principle is simple: **security belongs in the Definition of Done—not as a last-minute activity after deployment.**

A working application is not automatically a production-ready application. Teams should be able to show what was tested, what remains unverified and who owns remediation.
